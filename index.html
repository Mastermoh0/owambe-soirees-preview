What kind of celebrations do you plan?+

Our advertised occasions include weddings, kamu, birthdays, naming ceremonies and bridal showers. Tell us about your occasion so we can discuss the right support.

What if I haven’t decided on a date or venue?+

You can leave those details open in your enquiry. Share what you know and what you’re still exploring.

Can I enquire just about coordination?+

Yes. On-the-day coordination is one of our advertised services. Select it in your enquiry and we can discuss the details and scope.

How do I find out about pricing and availability?+

Send your event type, preferred date and the support you need. Contact the team directly to discuss pricing and availability for your occasion.
